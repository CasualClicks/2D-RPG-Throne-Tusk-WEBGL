# 🛡️ 2D RPG: Throne Tusk

**[▶ Play the Game (WebGL Build)](https://casualclicks.github.io/2D-RPG-Throne-Tusk-WEBGL/)**

A lightweight 2D RPG game made in Unity, where your goal is to **survive enemy waves** and **protect your King**. Command your units, build defenses, and outlast the orc onslaught!

---

## 🎮 Gameplay Overview

Throne Tusk is a real-time strategy-style RPG featuring:

### 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 **Ally Units**
- **Warrior, Archer** – Melee and ranged attackers.
- **King** – Your main unit; protect him at all costs.
- **Worker** – Gathers resources and builds defenses.

### 👹 **Enemy Units**
- **Orcs** – Standard melee attackers.
- **Bombers** – Explosive enemies that deal heavy area damage.

### 🛠️ **Core Mechanics**
- **Click to Select/Unselect** units.
- **Action Menu** appears for selected units:
  - **Workers**:  
    - Chop trees (collect wood)  
    - Mine gold  
    - Build towers (e.g., Archer Tower)
  - **Other Units**:  
    - Switch between **Attack** or **Defend** stance.

- Survive wave-based attacks and **maintain your army** to protect your throne.

---

## 🧪 Features

- Fully interactive UI for unit control.
- Resource management (Wood & Gold).
- Tower building mechanic with defensive advantage.
- Unit stance switching.
- Basic AI behaviors and pathfinding.
- WebGL-ready for browser play.
